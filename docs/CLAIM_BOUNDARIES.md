# Claim boundaries

## Supported

- The operator and equation insertions are explicit and reproducible.
- Baseline recovery, deterministic replay, numerical convergence, finite-grid leakage, and matched controls are recorded where applicable.
- In the published model-product and simulation-parametric diagnostics, the frozen redistribution operator often reduced the selected residual metric.
- The HFF map direction is explained by generic low-pass redistribution under the present comparison.
- The fresh Halo352 result is a clean directional diagnostic and a clean Plummer-specificity failure.
- In the SPARC shared-adapter heldout lane, the frozen Plummer adapter reduced
  aggregate chi-square by 9.256213 (4.432%) and improved 5/6 stable galaxies.

## Conditional / limited

- AS1063 B2 shows a 19.857% public model-component residual reduction, but generic controls recover more than 94% of the gain.
- Cross-cluster HFF directions are six physical targets but share model teams, processing, and score design; they are not six methodologically independent experiments.
- The 2D and 3D kernels have different lineages and are not one identical physical operator.
- The SPARC heldout status applies to shared `f/eta` only. Every heldout galaxy
  still received a fitted NFW baseline, one of seven baselines was unstable,
  and the 5/6 sign result is not confirmatory (`p = 0.109375` under an
  independent equal-sign null).
- Plummer beat the two frozen matched generic controls in SPARC aggregate
  chi-square, but Gaussian and top-hat also improved 5/6 stable galaxies.
  Kernel-specific physics remains unproven.

## Not supported

- Detection of an orthogonal source, hidden sheet, or new stress-energy component.
- Plummer-specific physics.
- Einstein-equation or cosmological proof.
- Replacement or falsification of GR, LambdaCDM, NFW, or baryonic countermodels.
- Raw-observational-likelihood improvement from model-to-model diagnostic scores.
- Treating overlapping channels or repeated development runs as independent validations.

## Public sentence

> A frozen orthogonal redistribution term reduced several frozen model-to-model residual metrics, but matched generic smoothers reproduced or exceeded the strongest gains; the current result is a reproducible generic redistribution diagnostic, not evidence for a new physical term.

The SPARC-specific sentence is:

> A development-selected galaxy-scaled redistribution adapter reduced aggregate residuals on a heldout SPARC subset by 4.432%, while matched generic smoothers also improved the same 5/6 stable galaxies; this is a shared-adapter diagnostic, not clean predictive or physical proof.
